FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Is Man Power an HRMS like Keka or HROne?

No. Man Power is a plant manpower and shift system: Excel team setup, Auto Schedule, coverage checks, leave/OD, and mobile roster views. It is not a full hire-to-retire HR + payroll suite.

Who is it for?

Plant managers, shift supervisors, and ops teams in factories, flexible packaging, printing, converting, and any multi-section / multi-machine environment that still runs on Excel rosters.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Man Power is a PWA with an offline queue so schedule work can continue on weak plant networks and sync when connectivity returns.

How is coverage different from a normal shift calendar?

After Auto Schedule, the app counts people per shift inside Section, Machine, Responsibility, and Designation groups. Short days are listed so you can change weekly offs and regenerate — instead of discovering gaps on the floor.

Can we still use greytHR / Keka for payroll?

Yes. Many teams keep payroll/compliance in their HRMS and use Man Power specifically for shop-floor roster quality. They solve different jobs.
