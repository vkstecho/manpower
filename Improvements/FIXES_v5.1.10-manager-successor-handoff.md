# v5.1.10 — Mandatory successor when Manager-with-team leaves

## Rule
When a **Manager who still has team members** accepts a join/transfer request to another team:
1. They **cannot** accept until they pick another team member (valid 10-digit mobile) as the **new Manager**.
2. That successor gets Manager role + team roster + shift config pointer.
3. Old members stay under the successor — never orphaned.
4. Leaving manager then joins the **requesting** manager as a **member**.

## If no eligible mobile on team
Accept is blocked with instructions to add a mobile on Team first.

## Home
Pending transfer requests show a banner with Review/Accept and Decline.
