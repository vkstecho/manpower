# 2.5.70 — My Shift click-drag multi-select

## Feature
On **My Shift** calendar (own calendar + Manager viewing a member):

1. **Click and drag** across day boxes → selects a continuous range
2. Orange outline shows selected days
3. Bulk shift bar appears → apply D/N/O/L/… to all selected days
4. **Single short tap** (no drag) still opens the normal one-day shift picker

Works for Manager and Member (with schedule edit permission for bulk apply).

## How to use
- Press on a day → drag across other days → release
- Choose shift from the bar at the bottom
