Privacy Policy — Man Power

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Man Power (“the App”) is a workforce shift and manpower tool for authorised company teams.

Data we collect

  • Account identifiers: name, mobile number (for login / OTP)
  • Employment data you enter: employee codes, shifts, leave, machine assignment
  • Optional: profile photo, holiday list images, uploaded Excel files
  • Technical: device identifiers used for session/device approval

How we use data

  • Authenticate managers and members
  • Display and save shift schedules and team records
  • Send operational notifications you trigger (e.g. WhatsApp templates)

Storage

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database / Auth / Storage) under the project configured by the organisation that deploys the App.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is visible to authorised managers and admins of your organisation within the App.

Security

Access is controlled by login and Firebase security rules. You must keep OTP and devices secure.

Your rights

Contact your company admin or support email to correct or delete employee records where applicable.
