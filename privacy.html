Privacy Policy

Mentor by VKS Tech · mentor.vkstech.com

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Effective for the Mentor web app, PWA and Play Store listing.

This Privacy Policy explains how VKS Tech (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you use Mentor — One-to-One Guidance at mentor.vkstech.com and related apps.

1. Who we are

Mentor is operated by VKS Tech (India). Contact: Care@vkstech.com · WhatsApp: +91-8929394920 · Website: vkstech.com

2. Information we collect

  • Phone number — for login via OTP (Firebase Phone Authentication).
  • Session / learning data — topics you unlock, bookings, notes, chat messages related to mentorship, and payment references (e.g. Razorpay Payment ID / UTR) that you submit.
  • Technical data — basic device/browser info, IP (via hosting/Firebase logs), and install status if you use the PWA or Android app.
  • Optional contact info — if you email or message us (name, email, WhatsApp).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not require access to your contacts, camera, or files unless you choose to upload something yourself.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your Mentor account (OTP login).
  • To deliver mentorship features: catalogue, bookings, recordings, doubt chat, and admin tools for the mentor.
  • To verify payments you report and unlock paid sessions.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

4. Payments

Paid sessions may be paid through Razorpay payment links/pages. Card/UPI details are handled by Razorpay, not stored on our servers. You may paste a Payment ID or UTR in the app so the mentor can confirm your payment.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database (account and app data).
  • Razorpay — payment processing when you pay via their link.
  • Hosting / CDN — to serve the website and app files.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and applicable law.

6. Data storage and security

Data is stored using Firebase and standard cloud hosting. We use HTTPS and provider security features. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Data retention

We keep account and mentorship data while your account is active and as needed for mentorship, legal, or dispute purposes. You may request deletion (see below).

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including India’s IT rules / DPDP where applicable), you may request:

  • Access to data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and related data

Email Care@vkstech.com from your registered context (include your login mobile number). We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Mentor is aimed at adults and working professionals. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where required). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Android app / PWA

The Play Store app and “Add to Home Screen” PWA load content from mentor.vkstech.com. The same policy applies. The app may use network access to provide login and mentorship features.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
