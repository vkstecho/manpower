Quick lesson 1 — Shift handover note

Write 5 lines every shift change:

1) Output vs plan
2) Quality / hold lots
3) Manpower present vs required
4) Machine issues open
5) Handover to next shift (name)
Quick lesson 2 — Escalate with data

Before calling the manager, capture: what, since when, impact (kg / hours), what you already tried.
