From spreadsheet to live roster

Your Excel is the system of record — we don’t force a new language.

  • Import team with section, machine, responsibility, designation, weekly off
  • Approve a member once — they land on Create Schedule without a second “add employee” step
  • Multi-select filters: Department, Machine, Designation, Responsibility
  • Hindi + English for supervisors and members

Typical plant flow

  1. Manager uploads / updates Excel roster
  2. Members join with mobile OTP (pending until approve)
  3. Run Auto Schedule for the week/month
  4. Fix coverage warnings → Save
  5. Team sees My Shift on phone
Coverage that HRMS calendars skip

Not just “who is on D or N” — is the machine covered?

  • Example: 3 people in one section + 3 active shifts → every working day needs coverage on each shift
  • Same rule machine-wise, responsibility-wise, designation-wise
  • Modal shows which groups/days are short
  • Suggest weekly-off changes, apply, and re-generate

FAQ

Questions plant teams ask

Is Man Power a full HRMS?

No. It is a premium rostering tool — Excel in, Auto Schedule, coverage by department/machine/role, My Shift on phone. Keep greytHR / Keka / payroll where they belong.

How fast can we go live?

Most teams start from an existing Excel roster. Upload, map sections, run Auto Schedule, and members see shifts on mobile the same day — not a six-month HR project.

What does the free trial include?

New users get a free trial after login (days set by Admin). Use real shifts on your phone. If it saves time, continue on a paid plan that matches your headcount.

How is this different from a shift calendar?

After Auto Schedule, the app counts people per shift inside Department, Machine, Designation, and Responsibility groups and flags short days — so gaps show before the floor feels them.

Can we still use greytHR / Keka for payroll?

Yes. Man Power focuses on rostering and coverage. Keep your existing payroll / attendance HRMS if you already use one.

What if it doesn’t fit our plant?

Start with the free trial after login. Use real shifts on your phone. If after the trial the roster still creates more work than it saves, you simply don’t continue — no long contract and no pressure.

Can we talk before paying?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute setup call on WhatsApp. We walk through your Excel roster, sections, and machines — no payment needed for the call.
